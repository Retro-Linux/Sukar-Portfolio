About

The Artist

A multidisciplinary artist working across graphite, oil, and digital media. Every piece begins with observation — the way light fractures across a surface, the tension in a resting hand, the silence between two colours.

The work is rooted in classical technique but driven by contemporary questions. What happens when traditional craft meets generative tools? When a sketch becomes a conversation with an algorithm? These intersections are where the most honest art lives.

Based in Amsterdam. Available for commissions, exhibitions, and collaborative projects.

  • 2016

    First Strokes

    Began formal studies in classical drawing and oil painting at the National Academy of Arts.

  • 2018

    Digital Frontier

    Transitioned into digital illustration, blending traditional technique with modern tools.

  • 2020

    First Solo Show

    Exhibited "Synthetic Dreams" — a 24-piece collection exploring the boundary between analog and digital art.

  • 2022

    International Recognition

    Selected for the Contemporary Visions Biennial. Began accepting private commissions worldwide.

  • 2024

    Studio Launch

    Opened a dedicated studio space. Focus shifted to large-format works and collaborative projects.
